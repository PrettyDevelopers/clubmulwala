Welcome to ClubMulwala

One of the region's premier clubs

Lake views, fine and casual dining, spacious bars, lounges and function rooms, modern decor and friendly service - ClubMulwala welcomes you!

Featuring 3 excellent dining venues, lake and balcony views, spacious and well appointed lounges and bars, the Club is spacious and welcoming and has something for everyone! There's live music each Friday and Saturday night, function rooms and catering packages, plus on-site 4 star resort with water views and the on-site Day Spa. The Club also houses an exceptional War Memorabilia room which is free to enter.

Members at ClubMulwala enjoy discounts throughout the Club and at our Resort as well.

Your Club

From RSL roots to today

During the early 1950's the Yarrawonga Sub Branch of the RSL pursued the development of a social club for the local community and RSL members.

The Mulwala & District Services Club was formed and purchased the property upon which the Club now stands. Its liquor license was granted on the 2nd August 1960. It was the Yarrawonga Mulwala Sub Branch of the RSL which saw the birth of Club Mulwala as we know it today.
